`Card` takes `desc` (a line under the title) and `className`, and has `Card.Header` for an unpadded card whose header holds more than buttons. New `CardGrid` (cards side by side) and `FormDialog` (a dialog that is a form: Enter and the submit button send it). From Hubra's Card, CardGrid and Modal, and Floorbit's Panel.
