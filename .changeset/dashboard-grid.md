Add DashboardGrid from Floorbit: boxes on a grid of whole columns and rows that never overlap and float up. Move them with a grip (drag or arrow keys) and resize them with a corner (drag or Shift+arrows). Rows can be fixed or fit to the screen, and boxes stack on a phone. The column count is the app's choice. The layout math is exported too (`moveInGrid`, `compactGrid`, `freeSpot`, `flowGrid` for turning an ordered list into positions, and the height snapping).
