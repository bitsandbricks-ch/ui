`NavCustomizer` and `useCustomNav`: people arrange their own sidebar. They pin entries to favourites on top, put entries away under "More", move entries between groups, rename headings and add their own categories. The prefs are plain JSON (`NavPrefs`) that the app saves where it likes. The pure helpers (`customizeNav`, `moveNavItem`, `toggleNavPin`, ...) are exported too.
