Action feedback from Seatbit: `ActionForm` (a form that runs an action, toasts how it ended, can ask first with `confirm` and save toggles with `auto`), `useRunAction` and `useToastAction` (the same for buttons and `useActionState`), `actionFailure`, `autoSave`, and `ActionSettingsProvider` for the framework's navigation errors (Next.js's `redirect()`) and error texts. A form whose action failed keeps what people typed (`installKeepTyped`, `markActionFailed`). `Notice` is a line that stays above a page in info, warn or err.
