AppShell: `navTop` (a scope picker or search above the links), `folded`/`onFoldedChange` (the sidebar folded to icons, with a button at its foot), `phoneNav` (a PhoneDock instead of the menu button on phones). Nav groups can fold from their heading (`collapsible`, `open`, `onToggle`), and nav items can open another site (`external`) or carry a button after the link (`action`, a star that pins the page).
