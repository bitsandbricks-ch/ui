`SidePanel` gets `mode`: `drawer` (the modal quick look, the default), `dock` (a column beside an editor's canvas) and `float` (over the canvas, not modal, so the page stays usable). Docked and floating panels share the drawer's head, body, foot and drag-to-resize, and become a sheet from below on phones.
