Navigation pieces from Seatbit: `SectionLayout` (a page's sections as rows on the hub on phones, a sticky sidebar on wide screens), `GlanceList` (the overview's rows with what is going on in each tab, a dot when something waits) and `Segmented` (SegmentedControl with counts, a corner dot and `fill` on phones). `PageTabs` gets `hidden` on a tab (only some people see it yet: muted, with a crossed-out eye and the reason) and keeps the active tab in view when the row scrolls.
