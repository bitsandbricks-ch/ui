Light `warn` (`#8a5700`) and `err` (`#b91c1c`) are a shade deeper and dark `faint`/`off` (`#8a8a94`) a shade lighter, so they pass WCAG AA as text on raised fills and, for warn and err, on their own soft tint (notices, badges). Seatbit's a11y tests found the old values at 3.8 to 4.46:1 there.
