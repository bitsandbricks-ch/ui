Inputs from Seatbit: `CopyField` (a read-only value with a Copy button), `ShareButton` (the share sheet on touch screens, copy and a toast elsewhere), `MultiPick` (a searchable multiple pick with quick picks, posting one hidden input per value) and `Fold` (a folded section that remembers it was opened). `copyToClipboard` copies with a fallback for browsers without the Clipboard API.
