List helpers from Seatbit: `usePagedList` pages a list the browser already has and keeps the page in range when a search shrinks it; `Pager` gets `showAll` and `onShowAll` for a "Show all" / "Show pages" button; `wordsMatch` and `foldText` search the way people type (case and accents don't matter, every word must appear, "mueller" finds Müller).
