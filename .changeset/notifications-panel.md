Notifications from Floorbit: `NotificationsBell` (the bell with its open count), `NotificationsPanel` (what needs a look in a side panel: filters with counts, search, paging at 50, the picked one's details beside the list, ↑ ↓ to step, on phones the details in place of the list until "Back"), and `NotificationDetail`, `NotificationTag`, `NotificationLink` and `NotificationLinks` for the details. The app brings the items and draws the details; texts use Floorbit's keys.
