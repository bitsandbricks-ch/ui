Shell pieces from Mason: `PhoneDock` (a bar at the bottom on phones, with "More" for every page), `SaveViewButton` with the `viewPath`, `addView` and `removeView` rules for saved views, and on `SidePanel` the optional `resizable` (drag or ← → its edge, kept in the browser), `onPrev` and `onNext` (step through the list) and `copyHref` (copy a link to its page).
