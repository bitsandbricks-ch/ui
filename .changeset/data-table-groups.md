`DataTable` takes `groupBy`: the rows split into groups, each under a header row of its own, in one table with one column header. The sort works inside each group, `groupOrder` sets the order and `groupHeader` what each header says. `foldable` (with `folded` and `onFold`) folds a group away. Paging counts the rows that show, and a group cut by the page break shows its header again.
